Evaluation notes

No qualitative score is generated automatically. Watch and listen before scoring realism, motion, prompt adherence, consistency, audio synchronization and cost efficiency. The speech test requires exact words and lip synchronization. The music test requires three landings with distinct synchronized footsteps and marimba notes.

Mean loudness is FFmpeg mean dBFS. Silence uses a −40 dB threshold with a 0.1 second minimum. These properties do not establish audio quality. See report.md, costs.csv, and technical_metrics.csv for evidence.
